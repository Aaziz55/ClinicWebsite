Who We Are

Excellence in Healthcare Since Day One

Al Barakah Medical Center is a premier healthcare provider dedicated to transforming the medical landscape in Somalia. Founded on the principles of Integrity, Compassion, and Innovation, we provide a sanctuary of healing that meets international hygiene and safety standards.

We specialize in world-class Ophthalmology (Eye Care) and Obstetrics & Gynecology, combining years of expert medical knowledge with a human-centric approach. At Al Barakah, you aren't just a patient—you are family.
